# Editorial voice

Status: **current owner-approved guidance**.

Post & In exists to elevate the profile of Seattle youth hockey. Its editorial
priorities are:

- **Discovery:** connect players with scouts and next-level programs;
- **Development:** give families clearer pathways and better information; and
- **Celebration:** tell the stories of players, coaches, and families.

Write with a confident, community-first, outward-facing voice for the broader
hockey world as well as local families. The premise is **Seattle is a hockey
city**, not that it is becoming one.

Avoid framing copy as a rejection of something else (a “not statement”). Lead
with the affirmative idea the reader should remember. Use the approved section
titles **The Arrival of Seattle Hockey** and **Focus on the People** when those
concepts appear; do not substitute “The Rise” or “People, Not Stats.”

This document owns durable editorial principles. Draft campaign or mission copy
belongs in an owner review rather than in current technical documentation.
